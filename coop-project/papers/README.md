# Papers: the counter-cyclical behaviour of cooperatives

These are four open-access papers on how cooperatives behave over the business cycle. Each one's licence allows redistribution without changes. The PDFs are the original files, not modified. All four are cited below, and anyone redistributing them must cite them too.

## 1. `magne_2026.pdf`

**Reference:** Magne, N., & Pérotin, V. (2026). When workers get to choose: Employment and wage responses to demand shocks in labour-managed and conventional firms. *Annals of Public and Cooperative Economics*, early view, 1–37.

- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/apce.70046
- **Source of this file:** HAL open archive, https://shs.hal.science/halshs-05559225v1 (the publisher's final published version, deposited by the authors)
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0. Checked in three places: (a) the PDF itself, which says "This is an open access article under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License" and on the HAL cover page "Distributed under a Creative Commons CC BY 4.0"; (b) the Crossref licence metadata; (c) Unpaywall, which lists both the Wiley and HAL copies as cc-by.
- **Summary:** The paper asks whether French worker cooperatives (SCOPs) adjust to demand shocks differently from conventional firms. It uses an exhaustive French employer–employee firm panel for 2005–2015 (about 7.3 million firm-year observations) and panel regressions of employment and pay on demand variation. In cooperatives, employment is more stable and pay is more variable, but the employment stability mostly benefits men and low-skill workers.

## 2. `reuten_2023.pdf`

**Reference:** Reuten, G. (2023). The Mondragon Worker Cooperatives' Employment Record 1983–2019. *Journal of Labor and Society*, 26(3), 336–375. (Published online 28 July 2022.)

- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1163/24714607-bja10080
- **Source of this file:** UvA-DARE repository, University of Amsterdam, https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/155270938/jlso-article-p336_003.pdf (the final published version with a one-page UvA cover sheet added at the front)
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0. Checked in the PDF: the article page says "Published with license by Koninklijke Brill nv ... This is an open access article distributed under the terms of the CC BY 4.0 license", and the UvA cover says "License: CC BY". The Crossref licence metadata agrees.
- **Summary:** The paper traces Mondragon's employment from 1983 to 2019 using the group's annual reports, in a descriptive longitudinal analysis. Mondragon's employment in Spain grew 3.4 times as fast as Spain's and 6.3 times as fast as the OECD's, including through the downturns. The paper also discusses jobs in subsidiaries abroad and the share of worker-members in total employment.

## 3. `sala_2015.pdf`

**Reference:** Sala Ríos, M., Torres Solé, T., & Farré Perdiguer, M. (2015). El empleo de las cooperativas. Un análisis comparativo de sus fases cíclicas y de su grado de sincronización. *CIRIEC-España, Revista de Economía Pública, Social y Cooperativa*, 83, 115–141.

- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.7203/CIRIEC-E.83.13421
- **Article page:** https://turia.uv.es/index.php/ciriecespana/es/article/view/13421
- **Licence:** CC BY-NC-ND 3.0. Checked in two places: (a) the article page links to creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/3.0; (b) the journal's "Derechos de autor y licencias" page (https://turia.uv.es/index.php/ciriecespana/es/derechos_de_autor_y_licencia) says "Los textos publicados en esta revista están bajo una licencia Reconocimiento-NoComercial-SinObraDerivada 3.0 de Creative Commons". The PDF itself carries no licence statement.
- **Summary:** The paper asks whether employment in Spanish cooperatives follows the business cycle and whether cooperatives act as an employment "refuge". It uses quarterly Spanish data for 1995–2014 on cooperative employment, GDP and total employment. It dates turning points with the Bry–Boschan algorithm and measures cyclical synchronisation with concordance indices. Cooperative employment is relatively out of step with the cycle, but there is no significant counter-cyclical refuge effect, and after 2007 cooperatives became more pro-cyclical.

## 4. `cantarero_2017.pdf`

**Reference:** Cantarero, S., González-Loureiro, M., & Puig, F. (2017). Efectos de la crisis económica sobre el emprendimiento en empresas de economía social en España: un análisis espacial. *REVESCO. Revista de Estudios Cooperativos*, 125, 24–48.

- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5209/REVE.56133
- **Article page:** https://revistas.ucm.es/index.php/REVE/article/view/56133
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0. Checked on the article page, which says "Todos los contenidos se distribuyen bajo una licencia de uso y distribución Creative Commons Reconocimiento 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)" and links to the legal code. The PDF itself carries no licence statement.
- **Summary:** The paper asks how the 2008 crisis changed the creation of social-economy firms (cooperatives and sociedades laborales) across Spanish regions. It uses annual counts of new social-economy and mercantile firms by region for 2002–2013 together with regional GDP, analysed with spatial econometrics (Moran's I and LISA cluster maps). During the crisis, social-economy firm creation fell and became regionally uneven, with a few spatial clusters, and traditional contextual factors (tradition, culture, regulation) lost influence.
